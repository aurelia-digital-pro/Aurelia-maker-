Title: The Return Signal
Language: en

Mira returns to the relay station after the first transmission fades. The mountain road is empty, and the old antenna field is buried beneath a thin blue fog. She remembers the warning in the first signal, yet every instrument is waiting for her.

The same wind moves through the cables, but the signal now answers. One light opens in the control room, then another, tracing a path across the dusty consoles. Mira places the copper receiver beside the map and hears a rhythm beneath the static. It is not a message from the past. It is a door opening somewhere beyond the ridge.

She follows the copper light into the archive and finds a map marked with her name. The coordinates lead to a place the station records have never mentioned. Before dawn, Mira sends a reply and leaves the receiver awake. At sunrise, the relay station becomes a promise rather than a warning, and the first clear signal returns from the valley.
