Title: The Last Signal
Language: en

At dawn, a lone rescue boat cuts through black water toward a failing lighthouse. Salt spray hits the lens as the keeper raises a red flare above the waves.

Inside a crowded night laboratory, a young engineer studies a pulsing radio waveform. Copper coils, glass instruments, and a hand-drawn map fill the table while the signal becomes clear.

On a high desert ridge at sunrise, the engineer reaches an old antenna and turns it toward the valley. The lighthouse answers with a warm beam, and distant boats change course toward safety.
