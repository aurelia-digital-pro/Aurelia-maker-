Example script for Aurelia episode

The sky is dark. We begin with the origins of the cosmos. The voiceover guides the viewer through deep time, star formation, and the emergence of elements.

This scene introduces tone and questions.

Next, we move to the formation of planets and the preconditions for life.

Then, the story zooms into the first living cells and the rise of complexity.

We end with the emergence of consciousness and the human story.
